Mode of Exchange C
is commodity exchange. This may appear at first glance to be a simple exchange of material goods, but that is incorrect. Here too an ideational power is at work—and it arises from ‘exchange’ itself. According to Karl Marx, exchange takes place with an unknown, perhaps dangerous other. Hence, the need arises for a ‘power’ to control the other—a ‘power’ of an ideational/religious nature. It is, in fact, what we call ‘credit’ or ‘trust.’
