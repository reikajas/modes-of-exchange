D.

Up until this point, I have been speaking only of three kinds of modes of exchange, but here I would like to introduce
Mode of Exchange D
. Strictly speaking, it is not one of the modes of exchange; it is a drive that seeks to negate ‘exchange’. It is not some imaginary being created through human desire or intention; to the contrary, it possesses its own ‘power’ of compulsion over humans. We can include literature and art among these. The ability of literature and art to escape direct determination by the productive forces and relations of production and to present a utopia that transcends these comes from
Mode of Exchange D
.
