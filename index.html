The Modes of Exchange, as defined by Kojin Karatani, are as follows: Mode of Exchange A, also known as Reciprocity, Mode of Exchange B, also known as Redistribution, Mode of Exchange C, also known as Market Exchange, and Mode of Exchange D, also known as Free Gift ☻.

What follows is a series of excerpts translated by Michael K. Bourdaghs from Karatani’s An Introduction to Modes of Exchange. Follow along.
